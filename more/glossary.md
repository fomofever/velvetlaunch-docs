# Glossary

**ATH ×**: how many times above the line price a coin's all-time high reached.

**Bonded**: the coin's curve sold out and it trades on its own pool, with liquidity locked forever.

**Bonding**: the coin trades on Velvet's bonding curve.

**Bonding curve**: the formula in the program that prices a coin before it bonds. Buying raises the price, selling lowers it. See [The bonding curve](../how-it-works/bonding-curve.md).

**Claim**: move your tokens from the line into your wallet (or take your refund if the line was called off).

**Cover charge**: the higher fee for about a minute after the doors open, paid to holders. See [The cover charge](../how-it-works/cover-charge.md).

**Creator bag**: the creator's opening buy, made inside the launch transaction.

**Dev burned**: the share of the supply the creator has burned.

**Doors open**: the moment the line ends and settles; trading starts.

**Free launch**: a launch where the creator only signs a message and the first buyer puts the coin on-chain. See [Free launches](../launching/free-launches.md).

**Guest-list minimum**: the least SOL a line must raise. Below it, everyone is refunded.

**Hold streak**: how long you've held a coin without selling. It sets your payout multiplier. See [Hold streak payouts](../trading/hold-streak.md).

**Holder pool**: each coin's pot of SOL that pays its holders every hour.

**In line**: a coin whose opening line is open. Commit SOL to buy at the opening price.

**Line**: the opening window where everyone commits SOL and then buys at one price. See [The line](../how-it-works/the-line.md).

**Line price**: the single price everyone in the opening line paid.

**LP lock**: a bonded coin's liquidity can never be withdrawn. Velvet Pools has no withdraw at all; for coins that bonded to Raydium, the LP tokens are locked forever in Raydium's locker.

**Velvet Pools**: Velvet's own swap program, where coins trade after they bond.

**Ready to launch**: a free launch waiting for its first buyer.

**Token-2022**: Solana's newer token program. Every Velvet coin is a Token-2022 token with its name, ticker and logo link stored in the coin itself and locked; no transfer fees, hooks or freeze. Coins made before Velvet switched are classic SPL tokens and work the same.

**Timelock**: the wait between proposing a settings change and applying it (48 hours on mainnet). See [Settings and the timelock](../security/timelock.md).

**vLvt**: the ending every Velvet coin's mint address has. The program refuses to create a coin at any other address.
