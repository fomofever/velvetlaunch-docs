# Opening buy and lock

When you **launch now** (not free), you can buy some of your own coin **inside the launch transaction**.

## The opening buy

* Up to **2 SOL**, at the coin's **starting price**, the lowest it will ever be.
* It happens in the same transaction that creates the coin, so nobody can get in front of you, and you don't compete with your own line.
* It's shown publicly on the coin page and preview as the **creator bag**.

## Locking it

You can lock the opening buy for **1, 7 or 30 days** (or not at all). A locked bag:

* sits in an escrow account controlled by the program, not by you;
* **unlocks gradually**, in a straight line, starting when the doors open;
* shows a **bag vests** badge on the board, so holders can see you're staying.

To take the unlocked part, press **Claim unlocked tokens** in the creator panel on the coin page. You can claim as often as you like; you get whatever has unlocked so far.

{% hint style="info" %}
If the line misses your guest-list minimum, the launch is called off. You can then undo your opening buy from the coin page and get its SOL back, minus the 1% fee it paid.
{% endhint %}
