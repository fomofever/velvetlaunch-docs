# Your position

With your wallet connected, each coin page shows **Your position** under the trade box, and a **Your P&L** line in the trade box itself. Everything is in SOL and USD and updates with every trade, including other people's.

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Headline** | Your unrealized profit or loss on what you hold, at the current market price. If you hold none, or your initials are out, it shows your total result instead. |
| **Holding** | Your tokens, including line tokens you haven't claimed yet and, for creators, the locked opening-buy bag. |
| **Worth now** | Your tokens at the current market price. |
| **If you sold it all at once** | What one sell of everything would actually return. A big sell moves the price down as it fills and the sell fee comes off, so this is lower than "Worth now". Smaller sells get closer to the market price. |
| **Put in** | Everything you paid for this coin, fees included: buys, your line spot, an opening buy. |
| **Taken out** | Everything your sells have returned. |
| **Still in** | Put in minus taken out. At 0 your initials are out, and what you still hold is free. |
| **Hold-streak rewards** | What this coin's holder pool has paid to your wallet. |
| **Total result** | Worth now + taken out − put in + rewards. |

Network fees (a fraction of a cent per transaction) and refundable account deposits aren't counted.

## Take initials out

Once your bag is worth **2×** what you still have in, **Take initials out** sells exactly that amount of SOL back to you, and leaves the rest in your wallet for free. Put in 1 SOL, bag worth 2 SOL: one tap gets your 1 SOL back, and you keep tokens worth about 1 SOL for free. It's in the Your position box, and as the **Initials** quick-pick on the Sell tab. Before 2×, the box shows how close you are.

* The sell's minimum return is your initials, so it either returns at least that much or doesn't go through. If the price moves up before it fills, you get a little more.
* The amount includes the sell fee and price impact, so you really receive your initials, not just their value at the market price.
* Once your initials are out, the box shows **0 · initials out** and your holding is tagged **free**.

## Sell by SOL amount

On the Sell tab, tap **⇄** next to the amount to switch between typing tokens and typing the SOL you want back. In SOL mode, type `1` and Velvet works out exactly how many tokens to sell to get you 1 SOL, fees and price impact included. The quick picks (25%, 50%, 75%, Max, Initials) fill in SOL amounts too.
