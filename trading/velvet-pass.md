# Velvet Pass

Every wallet that trades on Velvet gets a **Velvet Pass**: a record kept by the program itself, across every Velvet coin. It earns tiers, and higher tiers get perks. Nobody can buy or transfer a tier; it only comes from what the wallet has done.

## Tiers

| Tier | How you get it | Perks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guest** | Every wallet starts here | None |
| **Regular** | Join **3 lines** | Your [hold streak](hold-streak.md) starts 6 hours ahead |
| **VIP** | Join **10 lines** and put at least **5 SOL** into them | Pay **half the cover charge**; your hold streak starts 12 hours ahead |

A line counts when you first claim its tokens, if:

- you put in at least **0.1 SOL** (refunded SOL doesn't count),
- the launch went ahead (cancelled lines don't count),
- it was a real launch, with enough wallets and SOL in its line (so throwaway coins don't count), and
- it isn't your own coin.

## Flips

Selling while a coin's [cover charge](../how-it-works/cover-charge.md) is still on is a **flip**. A flip drops your Pass **one tier** (VIP to Regular, Regular to Guest) until you've joined **5 more lines** without flipping. Then your tier comes back.

## Where to see it

With your wallet connected, the trade box on every coin page shows your tier and how far you are from the next one (for example *Guest · 1/3 lines*). Your Pass is created the first time you trade or claim, which costs a one-time deposit of about 0.0011 SOL.

## Why

The line makes the opening fair, and the cover charge makes flipping it expensive. The Pass rewards the opposite: wallets that keep showing up, join lines and hold get cheaper openings and faster rewards, and wallets that flip openings lose that.
