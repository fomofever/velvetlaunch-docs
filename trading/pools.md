# Pools

The **Pools** page lists every coin that has bonded, with its price, 24h change, pool liquidity, 24h volume, what its creator has earned, and how much of its liquidity is locked (always 100%). Sort by deepest pool, volume, newest or biggest movers; **Trade** opens the coin page.

At the top, **Where pool fees go** shows how the pool fees the Velvet program collects are split, straight from the on-chain settings: the coin's creator, its holders, a share added back into the pool (locked), and Velvet.

Bonded coins trade in Raydium pools that the Velvet program opens and locks at bonding, so they're also tradable on Jupiter and in any wallet or terminal. See [Bonding to Raydium](../how-it-works/bonding-to-raydium.md).
