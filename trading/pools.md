# Pools

The **Pools** page lists every coin that has bonded, with its price, 24h change, pool liquidity, 24h volume, what its creator has earned, and how much of its liquidity is locked (always 100%). Sort by deepest pool, volume (24h, this week, this month or all time), newest, biggest movers, or **Top creators** (what each coin's creator has earned, all time, this week or this month). Every sort goes both ways: tap the order button, the open tab again, or the **Price**, **24h**, **Liquidity**, **Volume** and **Creator earned** column headings. **Trade** opens the coin page.

At the top, **Where pool fees go** shows how each pool trade's fee is split: part stays in the pool, and the rest goes to the coin's holders, its creator and Velvet. Pick **Velvet Pools** to see its split (0.20% stays in the pool, 0.05% each to holders, the creator and Velvet).

Bonded coins trade in their own **Velvet Pools** pool, opened by the program at bonding with the liquidity locked forever. See [Bonding](../how-it-works/bonding.md).
