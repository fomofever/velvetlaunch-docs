# Pools

The **Pools** page lists every coin that has bonded, with its price, 24h change, pool liquidity, 24h volume, what its creator has earned, and how much of its liquidity is locked (always 100%). Filter by pool (**All**, **Velvet Pools**, **Raydium**), and sort by deepest pool, volume, newest or biggest movers; **Trade** opens the coin page. Every coin's logo carries a small badge for its pool, and the **Pool** column names it.

At the top, **Where pool fees go** shows how each pool trade's fee is split: part stays in the pool, and the rest goes to the coin's holders, its creator and Velvet. Pick **Velvet Pools** to see its split (0.20% stays in the pool, 0.05% each to holders, the creator and Velvet).

Bonded coins trade in their own **Velvet Pools** pool, opened by the program at bonding with the liquidity locked forever. Coins that bonded earlier trade in locked Raydium pools. See [Bonding](../how-it-works/bonding-to-raydium.md).
